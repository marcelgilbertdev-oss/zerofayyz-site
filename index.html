What we do

Three things, done properly, rather than everything done adequately.

Game-ready 3D

Characters · environments · props

The part of asset production that eats weeks: taking a mesh and making it something your engine actually accepts. Rigged, skinned, animated, LOD'd, and verified in the target engine — not handed over as a blend file with a shrug.

  • Character rigging and skinning, including retargeting onto an existing skeleton
  • Named animation sets — locomotion, idles, actions — exported and parity-checked in engine
  • Environment assets and props, built to your game's scale and budget
  • LOD generation and export prep for Unity, Unreal or raw Blender
  • Companion and gameplay systems built around the character you're shipping
Why the engine check matters: FBX export can silently corrupt IK bones, and Blender's own importer will re-open the broken file looking correct. We learned that the hard way, so every rig now ships with a parity test that runs in the engine. That's the difference between an asset and an asset that works.
Want a finished pack instead of custom work? Our ready-made game assets are published under ZeroReign Studios — starting with LanternTail, a stylized creature pack with 31 animations, 16 colourways, swappable gear and 18 matching props, built for Unity and Unreal.

AI video & animated shorts

Production · direction · narration

Short-form animated and AI-generated video, produced end to end. Model selection is most of the craft — the same prompt gives you a usable shot on one model and garbage on another, and knowing which is which is the job.

  • Concept through finished cut, with characters that stay consistent across shots
  • Image-to-video pipelines using the right model per shot, not one tool for everything
  • Voice and narration, including custom voice creation
  • Assembly, grade and delivery in the format your platform wants
Public proof: the model-by-model prompting reference on this site is ours — ~15 image and video models compared side by side, free and open. It's the working knowledge behind the production work.
